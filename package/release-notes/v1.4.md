# Sound like the console

Every look now sounds like the machine it draws.

- **The music on each look's own band:** the NES's chip; a small SNES orchestra in a hall (brass, flute, harp,
  strings, timpani); a PC sound card's FM, played dry; a music box and toy glockenspiel on paper. Switching
  looks (L) keeps your place in the song.
- **The look's own sound effects** for the game's six sounds - picking up, dropping, a dragon's roar, being
  eaten, a dragon dying, the win - and two the 2600 never had: the castle gates rattling open and shut.
- **Each dragon has its own voice:** Rhindle high and quick, Yorgle in the middle, Grundle deep and slow. In
  the pbj pack the hen squawks and the Bog gurgles. You hear them stomping towards you.
- **A bright summer day with the music off (M):** a breeze and birds in the meadow, a woodpecker in the woods,
  still air in the castle halls, drips in the catacombs - and your own footsteps on grass, stone or snow.
  The pbj world adds a fire's crackle in the ember lands and a cold wind at Frosthold.
- **The 2600-only view (V) plays the original's sounds.**

For pack makers: a world can choose what each of its places sounds like (`ambience`), and a cast how each of
its dragons sounds (`voices`) - see [docs/making-a-pack.md](https://github.com/majonesmax/adventure-forever/blob/main/docs/making-a-pack.md).
